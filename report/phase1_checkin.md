# Phase 1 check-in: Case Study B, The 8 PM Problem

We chose **Bengaluru** and the question **"Does rain delay, flatten or worsen Bengaluru's evening PM2.5 peak, and on which weekdays and months is the evening pollution rush worst?"** It serves a decision the commissioner can act on: **when to issue evening pollution alerts**, and whether **rain-day rules for delivery riders and staggered office hours** need different timing. Our period is **1 October 2025 to 20 November 2026** (14 months, two Diwalis, both monsoons). We tested each source for our city and dates on 26 November 2026. (1) The **OpenAQ v3 API (JSON)** works: 25 locations in Bengaluru, and **7 KSPCB/CPCB reference stations** have hourly PM2.5 for 62–86% of the period (Jayanagar, Hombegowda Nagar, Hebbal, Silk Board, Bapuji Nagar, BTM Layout, Peenya). (2) The **Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (JSON)** works: 24 of 24 hours of rain, temperature, humidity, wind and boundary-layer height on our test day. (3) We are building a **Karnataka holiday and festival calendar** by hand from the central and Karnataka government holiday lists.
